Privacy Policy

Scholark is an independent, student-built educational project. This page explains the information Scholark uses to provide accounts and planning tools, what stays on your device, and the choices you have.

Effective: September 6, 2026 · Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. Information you provide

When you create an account, Scholark may process information such as your name, email address, authentication identifier, and information you choose to save in your account. Depending on the tool, that can include saved calculations, planning items, application-tracking information, goals, notes, or drafts you intentionally save.

Essay privacy: the Essay Rubric Coach is designed to run its rubric analysis on-device. If you choose to save or sync a draft, the saved content may be stored with your account so you can access it later.

2. Information stored on your device

Scholark uses browser storage for things such as preferences, recently used tools, progress state, and your cookie-consent choice. Necessary browser storage helps the site remember settings and keep core features working.

3. Service providers

Scholark uses third-party infrastructure to operate certain features. Google Firebase is used for authentication and cloud data features. Google services may also be used for advertising or related measurement where enabled. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

4. Cookies, advertising, and optional measurement

Necessary storage is used to run the site. Optional advertising or measurement storage is controlled through Scholark's cookie-preference controls. Until you make a choice, Scholark's consent layer defaults optional advertising and analytics storage to denied where supported.

You can change your choice at any time.

5. How information is used

  • Provide, save, and sync features you request.
  • Authenticate accounts and protect access to saved information.
  • Maintain site reliability, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors.
  • Send emails you have signed up to receive, with unsubscribe options where applicable.
  • Improve Scholark's educational tools and user experience.

6. Selling personal information

Scholark does not sell personal information. The project is not a data broker.

7. Security

Scholark uses HTTPS in production, account authentication, access-control rules for cloud data, and a repository secret-scanning policy intended to keep private credentials out of browser code. No online system can guarantee perfect security, so avoid placing highly sensitive information in educational planning fields.

8. Young users

Scholark is designed for students, including high-school students. It does not ask users to submit sensitive identity documents through the planning tools. If your local law requires a parent or guardian's permission for online services, use Scholark with that permission.

9. Your choices

You can choose what information to save, change cookie preferences, unsubscribe from optional emails, and use account controls made available in Scholark. If you want help with data associated with an account, use the contact method provided on the main site.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Scholark changes. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are published.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about Scholark, use the contact information provided on the Scholark website or project repository.
