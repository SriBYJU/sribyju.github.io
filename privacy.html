Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Scholark uses to provide accounts and educational planning tools, what stays on your device, and the choices you have.

Effective: September 6, 2026 · Last updated: September 6, 2026
S
Scholark is not a company

Scholark is an independent, student-built, non-commercial educational project. It is not an incorporated company, business, or employer, and it is not revenue-generating. It does not sell paid access, subscriptions, advertising, or professional services. The project exists to provide free educational planning and academic-support tools for students.

1. Information you provide

When you create an account, Scholark may process information such as your name, email address, authentication identifier, and information you choose to save in your account. Depending on the tool, that can include saved calculations, planning items, application-tracking information, goals, notes, or drafts you intentionally save.

Essay privacy: the Essay Rubric Coach is designed to run its rubric analysis on-device. If you choose to save or sync a draft, the saved content may be stored with your account so you can access it later.

2. Information stored on your device

Scholark uses browser storage for things such as preferences, recently used tools, progress state, and your cookie-consent choice. Necessary browser storage helps the site remember settings and keep core features working.

3. Service providers

Scholark uses third-party infrastructure to operate certain features. Google Firebase is used for authentication and cloud data features. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

4. Cookies and optional analytics

Necessary storage is used to run the site. Optional analytics storage is controlled through Scholark's cookie-preference controls and defaults to denied until you choose otherwise where supported.

Scholark does not use advertising cookies or advertising personalization, and advertising monetization is disabled for this non-commercial project.

5. How information is used

  • Provide, save, and sync educational features you request.
  • Authenticate accounts and protect access to saved information.
  • Maintain site reliability, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors.
  • Send optional emails you have signed up to receive, with unsubscribe options where applicable.
  • Improve Scholark's educational tools and user experience.

6. Selling personal information

Scholark does not sell personal information. It is not a data broker and does not operate as a commercial data business.

7. Security

Scholark uses HTTPS in production, account authentication, access-control rules for cloud data, and repository secret-scanning intended to keep private credentials out of browser code. No online system can guarantee perfect security, so avoid placing highly sensitive information in educational planning fields.

8. Young users

Scholark is designed for students, including high-school students. It does not ask users to submit sensitive identity documents through the planning tools. If your local law requires a parent or guardian's permission for online services, use Scholark with that permission.

9. Your choices

You can choose what information to save, change analytics preferences, unsubscribe from optional emails, and use account controls made available in Scholark. If you want help with data associated with an account, use the contact method provided on the main site.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Scholark changes. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are published.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about Scholark, use the contact information provided on the Scholark website or project repository.
