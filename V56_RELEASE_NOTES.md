# Scholark V5.6 — Observatory

V5.6 adds a new interactive Scholark Observatory between College Fit and the live tool grid, with four connected journey states (Plan, Prepare, Improve, Achieve), a controlled accessible carousel, 3D pointer depth, orbit geometry, floating paper layers, and direct live-tool CTAs.

It also introduces three new skill-driven patterns: the UI UX Pro Max complex split-headline treatment for a short hero line, the accessible auto-rotation carousel pattern (pause on hover/focus/offscreen/hidden/reduced motion), and the immersive-landing `Skip cinematic` path. The Brand skill is used to keep the new environment on semantic Scholark cream / burnt-orange tokens rather than introducing a new palette.

Existing clouds now react subtly to scroll velocity and the opening S gets a short speed bloom during fast camera movement. Mobile removes the expensive bloom/wind effects while preserving the full interactive Observatory controls.
