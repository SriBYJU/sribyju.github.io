# SAT/ACT release benchmark

Reviewed: 2026-08-15

This is an engineering and product-readiness benchmark, not an independent effectiveness study. The reproducible result from `prep-market-quality-audit.js` measures checked source, content, feature, interface, reliability, and testing criteria. It does not mean Scholark has been externally proven to outperform another platform or to raise an official score by a particular amount.

## Public Acely comparison

Acely's public SAT, ACT, and pricing pages currently advertise adaptive study plans, diagnostics, score predictions, progress and pacing reports, an AI tutor, 9,000 SAT questions, 5,000 ACT questions, 30 SAT practice tests, 20 ACT practice tests, answer elimination, bookmarks, Desmos, and paid score-improvement terms. Its limited free tier advertises up to 100 questions without hints, explanations, or practice tests:

## Five focused differentiators

1. Decision Profile Map separates fast/accurate fluency, careful/accurate work, rushed misses, and deliberate misses using the student's own median pace.
2. Confidence calibration identifies confident misconceptions instead of tracking accuracy alone.
3. Misconception Replay serves a fresh transfer question after a saved error.
4. The headline stability band is narrow after a complete registered form while the broader 80% model interval remains visible in methodology.
5. A deterministic verifier recomputes every eligible generated math answer before release.
6. Choice Diagnosis explains why each of the four answer choices is right or wrong without a paid AI call.
7. Item quarantine can remove weak questions from selection after enough aggregate, privacy-safe evidence.

## Remaining evidence gap

Without teachers, paid reviewers, or a recruited test group, the closest defensible release standard is official-public blueprint alignment, deterministic answer verification, automated cue/ambiguity audits, realistic browser simulations, and uncertainty-aware scoring priors. Scholark has fewer advertised questions than Acely and replaces open-ended AI with dependable structured tutoring. Operational SAT/ACT item parameters and equating algorithms are private, so official Bluebook and ACT practice should remain the final calibration check for a student.
