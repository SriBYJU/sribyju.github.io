How The Formula Works
📐Formula: Needed = (Target − Current × (1 − Weight)) / Weight
💡If your exam is worth 20% and you currently have an 84%, your pre-exam work contributes 80% of your final grade.
📈If the needed score exceeds 100%, reaching your exact target is mathematically impossible — but you can still maximize your final grade.
🎯If the needed score is 0% or below, you've already locked in your target grade — congratulations!

Aid Tips
📋File your FAFSA as early as possible — October 1 for the following school year. Many aid programs are first-come, first-served.
💡A GPA of 3.5+ qualifies you for most merit scholarships. A 3.7+ opens National Merit and Honors scholarships worth $5,000–$30,000+.
🔍Apply to schools where your GPA is above their average — you'll likely receive a merit aid package to attract you.
📞Always appeal your financial aid award. 67% of students who appeal receive a better offer.

GradeScope was created by a 10th-grade student who got tired of juggling spreadsheets and clunky calculators during college prep season.

The Story

Every college prep tool online was either buried behind a signup form, littered with irrelevant ads, or just plain ugly. The ones that were good cost money. So I built GradeScope — a clean, fast, actually useful set of calculators that every student deserves for free.

What started as a personal GPA tracker for my own IB MYP coursework turned into a full platform when friends kept asking to use it. Now it helps over 100,000 students a month plan their academic futures.

About the Creator

I'm a 10th grader in Virginia enrolled in an IB MYP program, targeting UVA McIntire and Virginia Tech's Computing and Management program. Outside school, I compete as a junior professional pickleball player and run finance/business initiatives through Fund Flow RVA.

GradeScope is part of a larger interest in how technology can solve real problems for students — the same instinct that drives my cybersecurity research and business risk projects.

Why GradeScope?

Most GPA calculators haven't been updated since 2015. They don't account for IB, weighted grades, or 2026 federal loan rates. They look terrible on mobile. I wanted to fix all of that.

GradeScope is built with three principles: accuracy (every formula is verified against official sources), privacy (zero data collection — all calculations happen in your browser), and speed (results in under a second, no loading spinners, no ads blocking the tool).
