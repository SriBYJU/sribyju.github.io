How The Formula Works
📐Formula: Needed = (Target − Current × (1 − Weight)) / Weight
💡If your exam is worth 20% and you currently have an 84%, your pre-exam work contributes 80% of your final grade.
📈If the needed score exceeds 100%, reaching your exact target is mathematically impossible — but you can still maximize your grade.

Frequently Asked Questions
Are all Scholark tools free? +
Yes. Every existing Scholark feature is free for signed-in students. Signing in is still required where Scholark needs to save or sync personal work.
Why do I need an account? +
Your account keeps saved calculations, essays, planner tasks, and applications associated with you so they can sync safely across devices.
How should I use the results? +
GPA, grade-needed, and loan outputs follow the displayed formulas and your inputs. College-chance and financial-aid outputs are rough planning scenarios, not predictions or offers.
Is my data safe? +
Calculations happen in your browser. Signed-in account data is stored under your Firebase user ID and protected by Firestore rules so other users cannot read it.

Scholark was created by Shriyan Avadhanula, an 11th-grade student building the college-planning platform he wanted for himself and other students.

The Story

Every college prep tool online was either buried behind a signup form, littered with irrelevant ads, or just plain ugly. The ones that were good cost money. So I built Scholark — a clean, fast, actually useful set of calculators that every student deserves for free.

What started as a personal GPA tracker for my own IB coursework grew into a platform used by more than 10,000 students each month.

About the Creator

I'm Shriyan Avadhanula, an 11th-grade student in Henrico High School's full IB Diploma Programme, with a 3.95 unweighted GPA and a 4.5+ weighted GPA.
