Frequently Asked Questions
Are the free tools really free forever? +
Yes. All 7 calculators are 100% free with no signup, no email required, and no time limit. Pro is for students who want the AI Essay Coach and extra power features.
How does the 7-day free trial work? +
Sign up with your email and you get 7 days of full Pro access — no credit card required. After 7 days, you'll be prompted to subscribe if you want to keep Pro features. You'll never be charged without explicitly entering your payment information.
How accurate are the results? +
GPA, grade needed, and loan calculations are mathematically exact. College chances and financial aid estimates are based on publicly available admissions data — they're directional guides, not guarantees.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription anytime? +
Yes, absolutely. Cancel any time from your account dashboard with one click. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.
Is my data safe? +
All calculations happen locally in your browser. Account data is stored securely. Payments are processed by Stripe — we never see your card number.

GradeScope was created by Shriyan Avadhanula, a 10th-grade student who got tired of juggling spreadsheets and clunky calculators.

The Story

Every college prep tool online was either buried behind a signup form, littered with irrelevant ads, or just plain ugly. The ones that were good cost money. So I built GradeScope — a clean, fast, actually useful set of calculators that every student deserves for free.

What started as a personal GPA tracker for my own IB MYP coursework turned into a full platform when friends kept asking to use it.

About the Creator

Shriyan Avadhanula, a high schooler in Virginia, enrolled in an IB program, targeting UVA McIntire and Virginia Tech's Computing and Management program. Outside school, I founded and run a data privacy organization called DataShadow to help people manage and monetize their data, and I've also filed a patent on specific AI claims, and run finance/business initiatives through Fund Flow RVA.
