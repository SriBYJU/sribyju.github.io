# Scholark Brand Guidelines — V5 Cinematic

## Source of truth
Scholark remains visually recognizable as Scholark. The redesign upgrades motion, depth, composition, and interaction quality without replacing the existing identity.

## Core identity
- Name: **Scholark** (capital S, lowercase k)
- Positioning: free, student-built college planning and academic support platform
- Independence line: **Independent educational project — not a company or employer.**
- Founder attribution: **Shriyan Avadhanula, Founder**

## Visual identity
### Primary palette
- Warm cream: `#FAF9F6`
- Paper cream: `#F3F1EC`
- Ink: `#1A1714`
- Muted ink: `#4A453E`
- Burnt orange: `#C8622A`
- Deep burnt orange: `#963C19`
- Warm highlight: `#E8A87C`
- Gold spark: `#EEB468`

The burnt-orange / cream relationship is the primary recognition cue. Purple/blue AI gradients, neon palettes, and unrelated dark-SaaS styling are explicitly out of scope.

## Typography
- Display: Playfair Display — editorial, academic, confident
- Body/UI: Outfit — clear, modern, compact
- Data/meta: JetBrains Mono — sparingly for labels and indices

## Logo behavior
Use the existing Scholark S/page/compass mark. Motion may separate the plate, paper-S, and spark in depth, but the artwork itself should remain recognizably the Scholark mark. Avoid substituting a generic S.

## Motion language
The motion benchmark is cinematic scrollytelling: one major pinned opening sequence, scroll-scrubbed camera movement, layered parallax for decorative elements, smooth section handoffs, restrained staggered reveals, and tactile hover feedback. Motion must use transforms/opacity where possible, preserve native scroll feel, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Visual atmosphere
Warm, optimistic, academic, tactile, editorial. Use procedural clouds, line-art campus architecture, paper-like surfaces, glass used sparingly, and soft sunlight. The page should feel illustrated and interactive rather than like a full-bleed stock photo.

## UX rules
- Minimum 44×44 px interactive targets
- Visible keyboard focus
- No meaning conveyed by color alone
- No hover-only interactions
- Mobile-first fallbacks at 375 / 768 / 1024 / 1440 widths
- Reduced-motion mode retains all content and actions
- Decorative parallax never applies to body copy or form controls
- One primary pinned scene; the rest should scroll naturally

## Voice
Clear, capable, student-centered, practical. Avoid hype that sounds corporate or claims outcomes that Scholark cannot guarantee. Prefer phrases such as “decision aid,” “planning tool,” “practice,” “compare,” and “verify.”
